🃏 How to Play UNO

Setup

Each player is dealt 7 cards. The remaining cards form the draw pile. One card is flipped to start the discard pile.

On Your Turn

Play a card from your hand that matches the top discard card by color, number, or symbol. If you can't play, draw one card — you may then play it if it matches, or pass.

Special Cards

  • Skip — The next player loses their turn.
  • Reverse — Reverses play direction. With 2 players, acts like Skip.
  • Draw Two (+2) — Next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn.
  • Wild — Play anytime. You choose the new active color.
  • Wild Draw Four (+4) — Play only if you have no cards matching the current color. You choose the new color, and the next player draws 4.

UNO!

When you play down to 1 card, click the UNO! button immediately. If another player catches you, you draw 2 penalty cards.

House Rules

  • Stack Draw Cards — +2 on +2, first player who can't stack draws the total.
  • Draw Until Match — Keep drawing until you get a playable card.
  • Force Play — You cannot draw if you have a playable card.
  • Seven-O Rules — 7 swaps hands, 0 rotates all hands.

Winning

The first player to play all of their cards wins the round!
