Official Rules & Strategy

How to Play Blackjack

1. The Objective

Your goal is to beat the dealer by having a hand value closer to 21 than the dealer’s hand, without exceeding 21 (which is a Bust).

2. Card Values

2 through 10

Count as their face value.

Face Cards (J, Q, K)

Count as 10 points each.

Aces (A)

Count as 1 or 11, whichever gives the best score without busting.

3. Hard vs. Soft Hands

A hand with an Ace counted as 11 is a Soft hand (e.g. Ace + 6 = Soft 17). If you hit a soft hand and exceed 21, the Ace automatically switches to 1 (becoming a Hard hand).

4. Natural Blackjack

An initial two-card hand containing an Ace and any 10-value card (10, Jack, Queen, or King) is a Natural Blackjack (21). It pays 3:2 immediately unless the dealer also has Blackjack (resulting in a Push).

Player Decisions

Hit (H)
Draw another card. You can hit as many times as you like until you reach 21 or bust (>21).
Stand (S)
Keep your current hand. Conclude your turn and wait for the dealer to play.
Double Down (D)
Double your original bet in exchange for receiving exactly one more card, then automatically standing. Available only on your initial 2 cards.
Split (P)
Split matching pairs (e.g., 8-8 or A-A) into two separate hands by placing an equal second bet. Each hand is played independently. Split Aces receive one card each.
Insurance
Side bet against dealer Blackjack. When the dealer's visible upcard is an Ace, you may bet up to 50% of your initial wager. Pays 2:1 if the dealer has Blackjack.
Surrender (R)
Forfeit half your bet to surrender your two-card hand before taking any actions if you believe your hand is unlikely to win.

Dealer Rules

The dealer has no strategic choices and must follow strict casino rules:

  • The dealer must draw cards on any total of 16 or less.
  • The dealer must stand on all 17s (both hard 17 and soft 17).
  • If the dealer busts (>21), all remaining non-busted player hands win.

Basic Strategy Cheat Sheet

Mathematically optimal plays against the dealer’s upcard (2 through Ace):

Hard Hands

  • 17 and up: Always Stand.
  • 13 through 16: Stand against dealer 2–6 (dealer "bust cards"); Hit against dealer 7–A. (Surrender 16 vs 9, 10, A if available).
  • 12: Stand against dealer 4, 5, 6; otherwise Hit.
  • 11: Always Double Down (or Hit if doubling not allowed).
  • 10: Double Down against dealer 2–9; Hit against 10 or Ace.
  • 9: Double Down against dealer 3–6; otherwise Hit.
  • 8 or less: Always Hit.

Soft Hands (with an Ace counted as 11)

  • Soft 20 (A,9) or Soft 19 (A,8): Always Stand.
  • Soft 18 (A,7): Double vs dealer 3–6; Stand vs 2, 7, 8; Hit vs 9, 10, Ace.
  • Soft 17 (A,6) to Soft 13 (A,2): Double vs dealer 4–6 (or 5–6); otherwise Hit. Never stand on Soft 17 or less!

Pair Splitting

  • Aces & 8s: Always Split (A-A and 8-8).
  • 10s (10, J, Q, K): Never Split (Stand on strong 20).
  • 9s: Split against dealer 2–9 except 7 (Stand on 18 vs 7, 10, Ace).
  • 5s: Never Split (Treat as Hard 10: Double vs 2–9).
  • 2s, 3s, 7s: Split against dealer 2–7; otherwise Hit.
  • 6s: Split against dealer 2–6; otherwise Hit.
