Official rules

How to play Gin Rummy

Make melds

Each player gets 10 cards. Form sets of three or four equal ranks and suited runs of three or more. Aces are low only. Cards outside melds are deadwood: aces count 1, face cards 10, and number cards their value.

Draw, then discard

Take the top stock card or visible discard, then discard one card. You cannot take the discard and put that same card back immediately. At the opening, the non-dealer and then dealer may pass on the first face-up card.

Knock or go gin

After drawing, knock while discarding if your remaining deadwood is 10 or less. Your opponent may lay off against your melds. With zero deadwood you go gin for a 20-point bonus and no layoffs are allowed.

Score the match

A knock scores the deadwood difference. A tie or lower defender total undercuts for 10 plus the difference. First to 100 hand points wins; official game, box, and shutout bonuses complete the scorecard.
